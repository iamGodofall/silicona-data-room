# SILICONA Commercial Pricing Standard

Status: PUBLIC COMMERCIAL REFERENCE
Effective: 2026-10-07

SILICONA separates platform access from variable EDA execution.

The subscription pays for the engineering platform.

Hosted execution is controlled by a server-rated execution credit schedule. Credits are reserved before execution begins and captured against the durable monthly credit ledger. Worker-side provider and infrastructure cost is recorded separately for unit-economics analysis.

Certification and private enterprise deployment are separate commercial products.

## Execution credit policy

Free includes a bounded $10 monthly execution allowance.

Engineer includes $100/month.

Pro includes $250/month.

Team starts at $750/month and is managed through a commercial scope.

Startup starts at $2,000/month and is managed through a commercial scope.

Scale starts at $5,000/month and is managed through a commercial scope.

Enterprise and Private execution, commercial EDA and private infrastructure are scoped to the workload and contract.

The launch rate card is versioned as 2026-10-07.v1:

| Execution class | Customer charge |
|---|---:|
| Standard | $5 |
| Heavy | $25 |
| Physical | $75 |
| Commercial EDA / signoff | $250 |

The execution class is selected by the server from the job type. Clients do not set or override the customer charge.

Credits are reserved before execution admission. Successful execution consumes the reserved allowance. Failed or cancelled execution releases the reservation. A request rejected before admission does not consume credits. Worker and provider COGS is tracked separately.

The credit allowance is a customer budget, not a provider cost claim. Actual worker, AI, storage and EDA cost is recorded separately and replaces planning assumptions as measured workloads accumulate.

Managed tiers are sales-led. Team, Startup and Scale are not self-service checkout plans.

## Enterprise model

Enterprise pricing separates platform software from variable infrastructure and EDA costs.

Customer-owned commercial EDA and BYOL are supported.

Private deployments price implementation, customer infrastructure, commercial EDA and SLA requirements separately.

Team, Startup and Scale are also contracted rather than provisioned through self-service checkout. Organization billing, named seats, SSO and deployment controls are scoped as part of the managed contract rather than presented as currently available self-service functionality.

## Why the model is structured this way

SILICONA does not sell unlimited cloud compute inside a flat SaaS subscription.

High-memory solver and physical-design workloads are bursty and materially more expensive than ordinary application traffic.

The commercial model therefore aligns customer price with engineering value while protecting SILICONA's gross margin.

## Gateway policy

Public prices are denominated in USD.

South African Paystack checkout uses an explicitly configured ZAR price-book conversion. No hidden or stale FX rate is used.

PayPal uses the published USD subscription price.

Annual Enterprise and Private contracts are sales-led.

## Claim boundary

Pricing is a commercial policy, not evidence of revenue, customer adoption or profitability.

Actual unit economics are measured from SILICONA's execution and AI cost ledgers and should replace planning assumptions as external workloads accumulate.
